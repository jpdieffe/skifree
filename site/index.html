ONLINE / SKIER + GUNNER Host Online Yeti Onslaught and share your room code or invitation. Your friend joins from their phone as the gunner. Skier: steer, jump, and collect weapon crates. Gunner: drag the aim pad to aim and fire, or touch a target on the slope; tap unlocked weapons to switch. Both phones show the mountain. Different networks are fine; no computer needed. Either player can open the menu to pause.

DESKTOP / YETI ONSLAUGHT Share one computer: player one skis with arrows or WASD and jumps with Space; player two aims and holds the left mouse button to shoot. Ski over numbered crates to unlock and equip weapons. Keys 1–6 select pistol, machine gun, shotgun, sniper rifle, rocket launcher, and plasma rifle. Unlimited ammo. Clear three waves per stage, then head deeper into the mountain. Open the menu to pause.

Controls & connection help

Phone: drag the ski pad to steer; up to brake, down to tuck. Use the big buttons to jump or change speed. Play portrait or landscape.

Keyboard: ← → or A/D steer · ↑ or W brake · ↓ or S tuck · Space jumps; E swings the solo ball. Both players share a view of the chain.

Keep both game tabs open. On Android, allow your browser’s Nearby devices / Local network permission if needed. Direct connections can fail on restricted networks; a TURN relay can help.
